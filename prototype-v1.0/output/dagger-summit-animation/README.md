# The Summit of Ambition — Dagger animation

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. The scene automatically travels from the Lower Bastions across the blade-crowned summit to the Eastern Ravines, then returns in one continuous 42-second loop.

The quotation “The strong do what they can and the weak do what they must” — Thucydides appears after the opening title and repeats in sync with the panoramic journey.

Controls:

- Use the bottom-left button or `Space` to pause and continue.
- Use the circular-arrow button or `R` to restart from the lower bastions.
- Pointer movement adds a slight depth response.
- The scene becomes a still panorama when the operating system requests reduced motion.

## Generated artwork

The summit panorama was created with the built-in image-generation tool using this production prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-screen panoramic backdrop for a looping side-to-side animation in the Ludus Fati world. The Summit of Dagger Ambition: an overwhelming high-altitude citadel where power, hierarchy, rivalry, discipline, and relentless ascent have been made architectural. An immense mountain stronghold spanning far beyond both edges of frame; steep switchback roads, training courts, furnaces, lower bastions, a colossal summit throne-fortress crowned by a blade-shaped obelisk, victory terraces, iron bridges, war-banner galleries, watchtowers, and cloud-filled ravines. Highly detailed hand-printed linocut and copperplate engraving fused with architectural concept art; tactile greyish-beige parchment, crisp charcoal linework, deep scarlet and oxblood ink washes, restrained brass and steel highlights. A very wide composition for a slow camera pan, with detail at both edges and layered depth. Hard dawn above storm clouds; severe confidence, hunger, challenge, and victory at a cost. No text, logos, border, watermark, cyberpunk, cartoon villainy, gore, or spaceships.

The animation layers are original HTML, CSS, canvas, and JavaScript with no external runtime dependencies.
