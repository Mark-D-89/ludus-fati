# The Restored World — Candle animation

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. The scene automatically travels from the Ruined Quarter through the central beacon and communal rebuilding works to the Living Gardens, then returns in one continuous 42-second loop.

The quotation “When we are no longer able to change a situation, we are challenged to change ourselves” — Viktor Frankl appears after the opening title and repeats in sync with the panoramic journey.

Controls:

- Use the bottom-left button or `Space` to pause and continue.
- Use the circular-arrow button or `R` to restart from the ruined quarter.
- Pointer movement adds a slight depth response.
- The scene becomes a still panorama when the operating system requests reduced motion.

## Generated artwork

The restoration panorama was created with the built-in image-generation tool using this production prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-screen panoramic backdrop for a looping side-to-side animation in the Ludus Fati world. The Epitome of Candle Hope and Mercy: a great vision of a ruined world being patiently restored through care, duty, endurance, and shared human work. At the far left, a war-scarred district at blue-grey dawn with broken arches, flooded streets, ash, abandoned machinery, and survivors carrying the first lanterns; across the center, ordinary people repair a monumental bridge and civic hall around a communal beacon flame, with field hospitals, shared kitchens, scaffolds, water channels, and gardens returning through cracks; at the far right, restored homes, orchards, clean rivers, reopened schools, windmills, and warm windows extend toward sunrise while some ruins remain as memorials. Highly detailed hand-printed linocut and copperplate engraving fused with sublime architectural concept art; greyish-beige parchment, charcoal linework, restrained amber, candle-gold, umber, ember-orange, and small green accents. A very wide panorama for a slow camera pan, with rich detail at both edges and layered depth. Tender courage, humble persistence, grief carried into renewal, and mercy without sentimentality. No text, logos, border, watermark, magical savior, glossy utopian sterility, active combat, gore, or photorealism.

The animation layers are original HTML, CSS, canvas, and JavaScript with no external runtime dependencies.
