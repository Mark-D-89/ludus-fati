# The Eternal Depth — Abyss animation

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. The scene automatically travels from the Last Observatory across the black sea and immeasurable central chasm to the Broken Causeway, then returns in one continuous 42-second loop.

The quotation “We live on a placid island of ignorance in the midst of black seas of infinity” — H. P. Lovecraft appears after the opening title and repeats in sync with the panoramic journey.

Controls:

- Use the bottom-left button or `Space` to pause and continue.
- Use the circular-arrow button or `R` to restart from the last observatory.
- Pointer movement adds a slight depth response.
- The scene becomes a still panorama when the operating system requests reduced motion.

## Generated artwork

The Abyss panorama was created with the built-in image-generation tool using this production prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-screen panoramic backdrop for a looping side-to-side animation in the Ludus Fati world. The Eternal Depth of the Abyss: a metaphysical black sea and immeasurable chasm where cosmic scale, decay, futility, alienation, and cold lucidity become landscape. At the far left, a tiny weathered island with the last ruined observatory and fragile lamps; across the center, an infinite obsidian sea parts into a colossal bottomless abyss beneath a black eclipse, with impossible inward spirals, drowned towers, torn horizons, and dead stars reflected in the water; at the far right, shattered cliffs and a broken causeway vanish into fog and negative space. Highly detailed hand-printed linocut, etching, and copperplate engraving fused with sublime cosmic concept art; charcoal-black ground, parchment-white and ash-grey carved linework, sparse cold indigo and moon-silver washes. A very wide panorama for a slow camera pan, with detail at both edges and layered depth. Eclipse light, cold silence, metaphysical dread, mourning, and vastness without spectacle. No text, logos, border, watermark, visible monster, neon, gore, or bright sunrise.

The animation layers are original HTML, CSS, canvas, and JavaScript with no external runtime dependencies.
