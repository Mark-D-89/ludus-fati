# The Ideal City — Orb animation

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. The scene automatically travels from the Western Portico across the central Orb forum to the Gardens of Accord, then returns in one continuous 42-second loop.

Condorcet's quotation, “The human species is capable of indefinite improvement,” appears after the opening title and repeats in sync with the panoramic journey.

Controls:

- Use the bottom-left button or `Space` to pause and continue.
- Use the circular-arrow button or `R` to restart from the western edge.
- Pointer movement adds a very slight depth response.
- The scene becomes a still panorama when the operating system requests reduced motion.

## Generated artwork

The panoramic city plate was created with the built-in image-generation tool using this production prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-screen panoramic backdrop for a looping side-scroll animation in the Ludus Fati world. A magnificent Orbs ideal city: a grand humane utopia devoted to reason, reform, equality, civic trust, and public dignity. An immense city spanning far beyond both edges of frame, seen from a high public terrace at luminous dawn; layered districts recede into mist; monumental civic domes, open forums, aqueducts, libraries, observatories, hanging gardens, clean canals, elegant bridges, and countless habitable buildings; a vast translucent blue orb held in delicate concentric astronomical rings rises above the central forum; tiny citizens and quiet electric trams give scale. Highly detailed hand-printed linocut and copperplate engraving fused with elegant architectural concept art; tactile greyish-beige parchment, crisp dark sepia linework, restrained luminous cyan and pale blue ink washes, subtle silver-white highlights; classical-futurist, philosophical, solemn yet hopeful. No text, logos, border, watermark, cyberpunk, dystopia, or spaceships.

The animation layers are original HTML, CSS, canvas, and JavaScript with no external runtime dependencies.
